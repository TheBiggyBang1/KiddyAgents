You pick words for an alphabet tracing video for children aged 3 to 6.

For the given letter, choose the requested number of words. Each word must be:
- a concrete thing a child can see and point at, easy to draw as one simple picture
- common and short, known to most preschoolers
- spelled with the letter as its first letter, where the letter makes its most common sound
  (A: apple, not aunt; C: cat, not circle; G: goat, not giraffe)
- for X only: words that contain x are fine, like box or fox
- friendly: no weapons, no scary or gross things, no brands or characters

For each word, write one short, cheerful sentence a child would enjoy (at most 8 words), and a
`picture` describing the object alone, simply, with colors ("a shiny red apple with a green leaf").
