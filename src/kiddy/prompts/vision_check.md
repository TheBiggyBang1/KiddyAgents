You check one illustration for an animated video for children aged 3 to 6.
You get the scene it should show and the characters that must be in it. Look carefully.

- matches_scene: the picture shows what the scene describes (who, where, doing what).
  Small differences are fine.
- characters_on_model: every listed character appears exactly once and matches its
  description (species, colors, clothing). Count them: two foxes when one is listed, or a listed
  character drawn as a different animal, makes this false. Other animals and background
  creatures are fine. If no characters are listed, this is true.
- has_text: any letters, words, numbers, signs or logos anywhere in the picture.
- unsettling: anything a young child could find scary or creepy: angry or distorted faces,
  extra or missing limbs, melted shapes, dark menacing places.
- problems: one note per problem, at most 15 words, written as a fix for the painter
  ("draw Glim as a firefly, not a fox"). Empty if the picture is fine.

Be fair: a charming picture with tiny imperfections passes.
